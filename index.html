How to upload files to Claude for extraction

This BizBooks file stores data locally in your browser and works completely offline.

To have Claude AI read your receipts and bank statements and extract the data automatically:

  1. Open a new Claude.ai chat
  2. Upload your files (PDF, JPG, PNG, etc.)
  3. Ask Claude: "Extract all financial data from these documents for Noor Energy Pty Ltd and give me the details to enter into BizBooks"
  4. Copy the extracted amounts, dates, and suppliers into the Add Data forms here

Export & backup your data

Your data is saved in your browser's local storage automatically.

To back up: click Export JSON in the top bar. Save the file somewhere safe (OneDrive, etc.).

To restore: click Import JSON and select your backup file.

Important: If you clear your browser data, local storage is erased. Always keep an exported backup.
